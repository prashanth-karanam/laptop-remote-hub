# 🚀 Laptop Remote Hub 24/7 & Central Portal

> **Turn your Windows PC into a high-performance 24/7 Remote Desktop with zero setup. Access, monitor, and control your laptop from any phone anywhere in the world at 60 FPS Ultra HD.**

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights

- **⚡ Zero Setup (1-Click Universal)**: Auto-installs Python, dependencies, and tunnels with a single click.
- **💎 60 FPS Ultra-HD Screen Mirroring**: Native 1080p / 4K resolution with WebSocket binary frame pipeline.
- **🌐 24/7 Central Web Portal**: Single permanent bookmark for your phone — auto-discovers online computers so you never have to copy-paste links again.
- **📱 Full Mobile Touchpad & Virtual Controls**: Mouse trackpad, smooth pinch-to-zoom (up to 5x), live phone typing, remote terminal, media keys, and power management.
- **🛡️ Private & Secure**: PIN protection, private registry channels, and bank-grade SSL encryption.
- **🔋 Windows Stay-Awake Sentinel**: Keeps Wi-Fi and CPU active 24/7 even with the laptop lid closed.

---

## 🛠️ Quick Start

### 1. Run on Your Computer
Double-click `START_REMOTE_24_7.bat`. The engine will start quietly in the background and publish its live session to your 24/7 portal.

### 2. Share with a Friend
Send them `LaptopRemoteHub_Portable_1Click.zip`. They just extract the folder and double-click `START_REMOTE_24_7.bat`.

### 3. Open on Your Phone
Open your 24/7 Web Portal, tap your active computer, and start controlling!

---

## 🛑 Stop Remote Hub
Double-click `KILL_REMOTE.bat` to terminate all background background services instantly.
